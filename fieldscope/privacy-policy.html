FieldScope

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026

FieldScope processes live sensor input on device to provide three visualizations:

  • Camera preview with body pose overlay
  • Audio spectrum and RMS levels
  • Ambient magnetometer readout

Data handling

  • FieldScope does not require account creation.
  • FieldScope does not sell personal data.
  • FieldScope does not use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • FieldScope does not upload camera, microphone, or sensor data to a developer-controlled server for core functionality.
  • FieldScope does not include user-generated social features.

Permissions

  • Camera permission is used for live scene preview and body pose overlay.
  • Microphone permission is used for local audio spectrum analysis.
